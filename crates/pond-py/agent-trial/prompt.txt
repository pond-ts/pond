`{DIR}/cpu.csv` holds CPU readings from three hosts (a, b, c), one every 5

`{DIR}/bars.csv` holds one-minute price bars for two symbols (AAA, BBB):

Write `{DIR}/solution.py` that reads those two files and writes four CSV
files into {DIR}:

1. `rolling.csv`, header `time,host,cpu_avg`: one row per reading of
   cpu.csv, sorted by time then host. `cpu_avg` is the average of that

   values), and `closed_by`, the 0-based position in the sorted feed of
   the reading that closed it. Rows in the order the buckets closed.
   Buckets still open after the last reading are not written.
