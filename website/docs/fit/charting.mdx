# Charting an activity

`toSeries()` gives the activity as a pond `TimeSeries`, and
[@pond-ts/charts](../charts/) draws a `TimeSeries` directly. So charting an
activity is the same as charting anything else in pond; this page shows the
three things activity charts usually need.

## Over time, with the laps marked

The interval run: speed on top, heart rate underneath, the six repeats
shaded. A lap's `fromSeconds` and `toSeconds` are seconds from the start, so
a `Region` for a lap runs from `start + fromSeconds * 1000` to
`start + toSeconds * 1000`. Hover to read both rows at one moment.

<ChartExample name="fit-run-chart" height={330}>
  {(width) => <FitRunChart width={width} />}
</ChartExample>

- **Speed is smoothed before it's drawn.** Speed worked out second by second
  from GPS jumps by a couple of km/h either way. A 10-second rolling sum of the
  distance steps reads as the runner's speed.
- **`origin="data"`** labels the time axis as time into the run, not the time
  of day.
- **Gaps break the line.** A heart-rate dropout is a missing cell in the
  series, and the chart leaves a hole there rather than joining across it.

## Over distance

A ride is often easier to read by distance: the climbs are where they are on
the road, whatever the speed. `byValue('km')` turns a series keyed by time
into one keyed by a column that only goes up, and the chart's x axis follows:

<ChartExample name="fit-ride-profile" height={280}>
  {(width) => <FitRideProfile width={width} />}
</ChartExample>

The six-minute café stop is invisible here: no distance passed. On a time
axis it would be a flat gap in the middle of the ride.

## Charts that come with fit

Some results are ready to chart as they are. Time in zones and the power
histogram come as bins with `start` and `end` edges, the shape
`<BarChart bins>` takes:

```tsx norun
const zones = ride.analyze({ athlete }).zones.power!;
<BarChart bins={zones} column="seconds" axis="time" />;
```

The analysis guides draw one chart each:
[moving time](./guides/moving-time), [splits](./guides/splits-and-pace),
[normalized power](./guides/normalized-power),
[the power curve](./guides/power-curve) and
[time in zones](./guides/time-in-zones).
